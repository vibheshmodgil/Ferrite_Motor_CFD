U666 benchmark motor: thermal CFD

OpenFOAM 12 conjugate heat transfer of the U666 benchmark motor, five duty points, to be tuned against the test data.

Key findings so far

  • Energy gate met on the non-conformal prism mesh: -1.14 % at 60 s (1.446 M cells).
  • The CAD housing could not be meshed; a simplified housing (-2 % volume, +1 % fin area) is used.
  • DC copper dominates the losses at duty1-3 (1133 / 548 / 324 W).
  • Duty1/2 done: winding limit 155 C reached at 4.1 / 13.2 min, magnets 100 C at 14.4 min in duty2 (see Results); the external-air h map is done (mean 3.95 W/m2K); duty3-5 and the test calibration are pending.

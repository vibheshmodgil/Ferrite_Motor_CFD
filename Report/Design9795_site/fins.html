Selected variant

Selected variant: tapered rings, H 16 mm, pitch 11.5 mm, full span with gland cut-outs: UA 1.51 W/K vs 0.94 W/K for the existing fins (×1.6), +0.47 kg aluminium.

The screen rates the best axial variant (axial H16 N68) higher, at 2.56 W/K (+0.70 kg).

Why rings. The axial-fin screen value rests on the least certain correlation. On a horizontal cylinder in natural convection the buoyant flow rises round the circumference: ring fins leave vertical channels open for it, whereas longitudinal (axial) fins block that circumferential flow, so real axial performance is usually far below the screen value. Rings are the robust choice and also suit the 4 → 2.5 mm taper. Axial fins will be tested with CFD on the real motor CAD.

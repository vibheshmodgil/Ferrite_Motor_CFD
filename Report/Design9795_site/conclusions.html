Conclusions

duty1 passes (winding reaches 155 °C at 6 min 28 s, target 5 min); duty2 passes (winding reaches 155 °C at 19 min 03 s, target 15 min); duty3 fails (magnets reach 100 °C at 22 min 18 s, target 30 min); duty4 fails (magnets reach 100 °C at 14 min 11 s, target 45 min); duty5 passes (no limit reached). The magnet-limited failures need combined measures on the rotor-side heat, AC copper and external cooling.

Limitations

  • Emissivity 0.7 on all outer faces while the housing is bare machined aluminium (ε ≈ 0.1): radiation was about 60–70 % of the external heat loss, so every time to limit is optimistic, most at duty4 and duty5 (duty4 re-run at ε 0.1 pending).
  • Mesh is the old v7 motor_65 geometry, not the Design9795_1 CAD: stack 65 vs 64 mm, shaft 30 vs 25 mm; outer diameter, bore and rotor match. Losses come from Design9795_1. Effect about 1–2 % on temperatures and run times.
  • Homogenised isotropic winding (k 1.0 W/m·K, uniform copper loss per volume) in the baseline; the anisotropic 0.77 / 200 W/m·K version diverged on the tet mesh. Heat cannot run along the copper from the end windings into the slot, so the baseline splits the winding heat about 50/50 between stator and end-space air, where the detailed model gives 69 / 31 % (duty1, 420 s). The baseline is pessimistic for the winding hot spot (190 vs 171 °C) and slightly for the magnets: verdicts are safe-side, but the heat split is not physical. Use the detailed coil or an anisotropic winding on the real CAD.
  • End-space air as a conducting solid from a correlation; the earlier internal CFD found h about half the correlation, so the end-turn hot spot is optimistic.
  • Assumed contacts (bobbin, shrink fit, glue); the bobbin layer controls the copper-to-iron path.
  • Still-air external h from one CFD at ΔT 60 K; no mounting conduction, no vehicle air speed. A dyno or vehicle mounting plate is a heat sink the model does not have (conservative).
  • Constant torque and speed per point; no drive cycle, nominal 52 V losses (battery sag raises copper loss, most at duty4).
  • Limit definition: the CFD limit is the hottest cell; a test thermistor in the end winding reads later.
  • Benchmarks: the Spoke (Ferrite) column is a different motor (multi-strand distributed winding, no busbar): a benchmark, not a prediction for this design.
  • Internal air at duty4 is an approximate solution (residuals ~1e-3); duty2 reuses the duty1 field scaled by speed.
